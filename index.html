About Me

Technology should speak to everyone.

My work sits at the intersection of Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, language, education and innovation.

My Story

From Computer Science to African AI.

I am a Computer Scientist interested in building intelligent systems that solve meaningful problems. My interests span Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, Generative AI, multimodal systems and emerging intelligent technologies.

A major part of my work focuses on the representation of African languages in modern AI systems, with particular interest in Hausa. I am passionate about developing datasets, models, benchmarks and applications that make AI more useful and accessible to African communities.

Beyond research, I enjoy teaching, building technology projects, creating educational content and exploring how emerging technologies can create opportunities across Africa.
