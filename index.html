My Story

From Computer Science to African AI.

I am a Computer Scientist interested in building intelligent systems that solve meaningful problems. My interests span Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, Generative AI, multimodal systems and emerging intelligent technologies.

A major part of my work focuses on the representation of African languages in modern AI systems, with particular interest in Hausa.
