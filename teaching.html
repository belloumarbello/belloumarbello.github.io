My teaching approach

I try to connect theoretical Computer Science concepts with practical examples and hands-on implementation.

Programming and Artificial Intelligence are particularly important to me because students learn more effectively when they can move from understanding a concept to actually building something with it.

I also support students beyond individual lessons through academic coordination, guidance, and practical learning activities.

Hausa-Language Education

Computer Science in Hausa.

I am interested in making Computer Science education more accessible to Hausa-speaking learners.

This has motivated me to create educational materials and programming lessons in Hausa while retaining important English programming terminology where it is useful for learners.

The goal is simple: language should not become an unnecessary barrier to learning how technology works.

Educational focus

  • Programming in Hausa
  • Python education
  • Computer Science fundamentals
  • AI education

Teaching Philosophy

Explain it simply. Build it practically.

I believe accessible education can play an important role in expanding participation in technology and Artificial Intelligence.
