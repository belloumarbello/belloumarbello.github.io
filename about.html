About Me

Building AI for people, languages & the real world.

I am Bello Umar Bello, a Computer Scientist, AI researcher, educator, and builder interested in creating intelligent systems that are useful, inclusive, and grounded in real-world problems.

Computer Scientist. AI Researcher. Educator.

My work sits at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, machine learning, education, and emerging intelligent technologies.

My Story

From Computer Science to AI research and innovation.

My academic journey is rooted in Computer Science. I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Science from Bayero University Kano in 2021, graduating with a CGPA of 4.1/5.0.

Over time, my interests have increasingly focused on Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, machine learning, and the development of intelligent systems.

I am particularly interested in how AI can become more inclusive by supporting languages and communities that remain underrepresented in modern AI systems.

This interest led to my work in African language technology, including research and development activities around Hausa and other low-resource languages.
