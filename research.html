Research Focus

African Language AI

One of my strongest research interests is making Artificial Intelligence more useful for African languages and communities.

I am particularly interested in Hausa and other low-resource languages, where limitations in datasets, benchmarks, language resources, and model evaluation can significantly affect AI performance.

My work explores the development and curation of language datasets, machine translation, language understanding and generation, speech technologies, and multimodal AI.
